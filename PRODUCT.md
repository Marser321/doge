## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Primary users are high-end residential property owners, luxury vacation rental (Airbnb/VRBO) hosts, and real estate investors in the Miami metro area who demand top-tier property maintenance, asset preservation, and flawless cleaning verification.

## Product Purpose

DOGE S.M. LLC provides precision eco-luxury cleaning services and real estate asset preservation. It streamlines service booking with photo/video request intake, transparent estimates, photo verification reporting, and an integrated professional cleaning store.

## Positioning

Eco-luxury precision cleaning combined with instant media-based request intake, photo-verified service completion reports, an integrated professional-grade product store, and priority membership tiers that no generic cleaning service offers.

## Operating Context

Miami real estate ecosystem; fast-turnover luxury rentals, high-end residential homes, and commercial properties. Service requests often happen on mobile devices with photo/video attachments, requiring instant estimates, GPS coverage validation, and verified photographic proof of work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual interface support (English / Spanish).
- No-friction booking: upload photos/videos of property or issues to get custom estimates.
- Integrated Pro Store for purchasing professional eco-friendly cleaning supplies.
- Membership tiers providing store discounts and priority crew scheduling.
- Internal staff CRM with role-scoped service history, scheduling, inventory, commerce and photographic verification.
- Supabase PostgreSQL, Auth and Storage behind a Next.js server-only business API.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DOGE S.M. LLC
- Aesthetic standard: "Estándar Profesional" / "Elite Cleaning"
- Eco-luxury focus, non-toxic professional products, precision execution, asset preservation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Next.js codebase with i18n dictionaries (`src/data/i18n.ts`).
- Versioned Supabase schema supporting staff, services, bookings, quotes, inventory, subscriptions and commerce.
- Transparent brand master (`public/doge-logo-transparent.png`).

## Product Principles

1. Flawless Verification: Every service completed must provide photographic/video proof of result.
2. Zero-Friction Booking: Allow users to describe needs with media instead of tedious forms.
3. Asset Preservation: Treat property not just as something to clean, but as an asset to preserve and value-enhance.
4. Integrated Ecosystem: Seamlessly link service scheduling, member privileges, and professional product purchasing.
5. Mobile-Native Ergonomics: Deliver a polished, fluid experience tailored for on-the-go property managers and hosts on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Bilingual ES/EN accessibility, high-contrast readable typography, full keyboard/screen-reader compliance, and responsive touch targets for mobile accessibility.
